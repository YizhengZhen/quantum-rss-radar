### Motivation

To address security risks in always-on AI agents where untrusted inputs can persist and trigger actions later through different surfaces.

### Method

The authors define a class of attacks based on persistence substrate and firing-separation, demonstrate a confused-deputy cron attack on OpenClaw, and propose a tiered defense (D1-D3) with a soundness theorem.
