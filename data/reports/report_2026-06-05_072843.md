### Motivation

The thermodynamic entropy of a subsystem is not generally a Lyapunov function in open nonequilibrium systems, raising the question of what quantity organizes irreversible relaxation. The authors aim to identify a thermodynamic quantity that monotonically increases during relaxation.

### Method
