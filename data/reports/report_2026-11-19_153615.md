### Motivation

Microwave-to-optical transduction is needed to link superconducting quantum processors over optical networks, but mechanically mediated transducers suffer from weak electromechanical coupling, dissipation and added noise; collective enhancement via qubit ensembles is a promising route to overcome these limits.

### Method
