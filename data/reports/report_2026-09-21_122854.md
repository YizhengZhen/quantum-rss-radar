This paper studies the optimization landscape of policy gradient methods for infinite-horizon discounted MDPs with general state and action spaces, establishing PLK conditions for global convergence under weaker assumptions.

### Motivation

Policy gradient methods lack global convergence guarantees in general MDPs because standard weighted policy iteration conditions can fail even when an optimal policy exists in the policy class.

### Method

The authors propose weaker conditions ensuring no suboptimal stationary points and establish the Polyak-Lojasiewicz-Kurdyka (PLK) condition with and without a concentrability assumption, then verify these conditions for inventory and stochastic cash-balance models.

### Result

They derive exponent-one and, under added curvature, exponent-two PLK conditions, yielding iteration complexity and linear convergence for projected gradient descent with exact policy gradients.

### Conclusion

The work provides the first non-asymptotic convergence rates for policy gradient methods on infinite-horizon discounted inventory systems with Markov-modulated demand and stochastic cash-balance problems.

*Keywords: policy optimization, Markov decision processes, Polyak-Lojasiewicz-Kurdyka condition, nonconvex landscape, convergence rates*
