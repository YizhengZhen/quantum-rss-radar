### Motivation

The paper addresses a safety concern in AI alignment, specifically the vulnerability of LLMs to abliteration attacks that remove refusal capabilities.

### Method

The method, AMRA, obscures the refusal direction by applying rank-constrained updates to residual stream writer matrices, replacing refusal-inducing activations with random aliases, and correcting downstream reader matrices.

### Result

On Llama-3-8B and Gemma-2-9B, AMRA improves post-abliteration refusal scores with minimal utility degradation.

The proposed defense effectively mitigates abliteration while preserving model utility, but it is outside the scope of quantum information research.
