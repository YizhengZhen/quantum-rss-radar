- **Tags**: `convergence`  `covert communication`  `cross-layer optimization`  `power control`  `distributed algorithm`

### TL;DR

This paper presents a distributed cross-layer optimization algorithm for covert wireless networks, addressing covertness constraints via a convex approximation and proving exponential convergence.

### Motivation

Covert multi-hop wireless networks require joint optimization of congestion control, routing, scheduling, and power control to avoid detection by adversarial wardens, but the detection error probability is non-concave, making optimization challenging.

### Method

The authors construct the tightest concave lower bound on the log-DEP to form a conservative convex problem, then develop a Parallel Proximal Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (PP-ADMM) algorithm to solve it.

### Result

They prove global Q-linear convergence to the optimal solution set under standard regularity conditions and demonstrate robust tracking under channel fading and Willie mobility with numerical results.

### Conclusion

The work provides the first distributed cross-layer algorithm for covert multi-modal networks with guaranteed exponential convergence, enabling efficient and secure communication in adversarial environments.

*Keywords: covert communication, cross-layer optimization, power control, distributed algorithm, convergence*
