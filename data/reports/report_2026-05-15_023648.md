To address security vulnerabilities in always-on AI agents where untrusted inputs can persist and later trigger actions through different surfaces.

### Method

The authors define two axes (persistence substrate and firing-separation) and implement a tiered defense (D1, D2, D3) with a canonical action-instance digest and one-shot owner attestations.

### Result
