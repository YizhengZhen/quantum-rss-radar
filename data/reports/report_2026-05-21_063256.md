## 144. Intersecting Dense Automata

- **Tags**: `nondeterministic finite automata`  `intersection emptiness`  `clique detection`  `transition complexity`  `algorithm optimality`

### TL;DR

This paper presents improved constructions and algorithms for the intersection of nondeterministic finite automata, reducing the number of transitions and achieving optimality under a clique-detection hardness assumption.

### Motivation

The classical Cartesian product construction for NFA intersection can produce a quadratic number of transitions, which is suboptimal for dense automata. The research aims to find more efficient constructions and algorithms.

### Method

The authors propose alternative constructions that reduce the number of transitions to O(n^2) for two NFA and O(n^k) for k NFA, and use a reduction from NFA intersection emptiness to clique detection to prove optimality.

### Result

The new constructions achieve O(n^2) transitions for two NFA and O(n^k) for k NFA, improving over the Cartesian product. The algorithm for emptiness is optimal unless there is a breakthrough for detecting k-cliques.

### Conclusion

The work provides a faster algorithm for NFA intersection emptiness and a more efficient certification scheme, with implications for formal verification and automata theory.

*Keywords: nondeterministic finite automata, intersection emptiness, clique detection, transition complexity, algorithm optimality*
