This paper introduces a Nambu nonequilibrium thermodynamics framework for open systems, demonstrating that a dissipation potential can serve as a Lyapunov function even when subsystem entropy is not monotonic.

### Motivation

In open nonequilibrium systems, thermodynamic entropy is not generally a Lyapunov function, raising the question of what quantity organizes irreversible relaxation. The authors aim to identify a thermodynamic quantity that monotonically increases during relaxation.

### Method

The authors construct an explicit open-piston model coupled to a pressure reservoir and heat bath.
