### TL;DR

This paper proves that the Gallai Vertex Problem is complete for the complexity class Θ₂ᵖ, settling its computational complexity.

### Motivation

The problem of determining whether a graph has a vertex contained in all its longest paths (Gallai vertex) had unknown complexity for decades, not known to be NP- or co-NP-hard.

### Method

The authors use polynomial-time reductions from problems complete for Θ₂ᵖ (parallel access to NP), such as the exact clique problem, to show completeness.

### Result

The Gallai Vertex Problem is Θ₂ᵖ-complete, implying it is harder than NP and co-NP unless the polynomial hierarchy collapses. Additionally, the longest path transversal number cannot be approximated within a factor better than 2 unless P=NP.

### Conclusion

This result closes a long-standing open problem and has implications for approximation algorithms for longest path transversal and related problems.

*Keywords: Gallai vertex, longest path transversal, Θ₂ᵖ-completeness, computational complexity, approximation hardness*
